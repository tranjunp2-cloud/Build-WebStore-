## Reflection

The strongest choice was making one validated generation artifact the boundary between stored data, JSON-LD output, and score calculation. It reduced divergence and made the 35-point contract deterministic. Effective-state validation also closed the subtle sparse-update path without requiring destructive migration behavior. Release confidence is high for the implemented scope, but Phase 03 should not be presented as complete until real staging evidence exists.

## Decisions Made

| Decision | Rationale | Impact |
|----------|-----------|--------|
| Version the subtype mapping | Schema vocabulary and product taxonomy evolve independently | Mapping changes are explicit, reviewable, and backward-compatible |
| Validate merged effective state | PATCH-style saves may omit the unchanged half of a dependent pair | API, admin, and direct Payload updates enforce the same invariant |
| Score only the canonical artifact | Recomputing from raw data could disagree with emitted JSON-LD | Score reflects actual eligible output and remains pure |
| Cap score at 35 with no FAQ points | v2.6 removed FAQ from this story's contract | Exact scoring contract is testable and cannot inflate through FAQ data |
| Keep Phase 03 pending | Fixture and Rich Results evidence are external prerequisites; #1708 reconciliation is complete | No premature release-complete claim |
