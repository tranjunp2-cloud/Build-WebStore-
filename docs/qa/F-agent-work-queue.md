Design: `plans/261022-proactive-assistant-task-sessions/design.md` (option B, revised 2026-10-22). "Issues" everywhere (was To do / tasks). One number of open issues on the mascot (the dashboard Issues card was later removed: issues live in the dock). A new chat greets as before and offers the top issues as reply pills; the header shows only the chat's name ("New chat" or the issue) with an Issues button (labelled Urgent / Needs you / Soon by the most pressing group), a History icon (the plain chat list with search and delete, as before) and an Expand icon only while a draft can be reviewed wide; no … menu, no Next strip. "Needs you" is the one word for waiting on the merchant (draft cards too). The mascot raises one issue at a time (✕ always visible, hides itself after 12 s; quiet 20 min after hiding, 1 h after ✕). Each issue is handled in its own chat. The site editor's chats stay in the editor.
