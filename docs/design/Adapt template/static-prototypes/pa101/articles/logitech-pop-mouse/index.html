The Basics of Fiddle Leaf Fig Care

A practical guide to light, water and the small routines that help this dramatic houseplant thrive.

By EcoGarden Editorial · 29 November 2018 · 8 min read

Fiddle leaf figs reward consistency. They prefer a stable position with bright, indirect light and enough room for air to move around their broad leaves.

Start with the right light

Place the plant near a bright window, protected from harsh afternoon sun. Rotate the pot every week or two so growth stays balanced rather than leaning toward one side.

Water with intention

Check the upper few centimetres of soil before watering. When it feels dry, water thoroughly and allow excess moisture to drain. Avoid leaving the pot in standing water.

Read the leaves

Dust-free leaves absorb light more efficiently. Wipe them gently with a damp cloth and watch for rapid yellowing, brown edges or leaf drop—each is useful feedback about the plant’s environment.
